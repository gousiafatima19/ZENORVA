About ZENVORA

ZENVORA is a student technology project built to support UN Sustainable Development Goal 2: Zero Hunger. It combines AI, community data, and everyday kitchen habits to reduce household food waste and connect surplus food with people who need it most.

Every figure shown in the app comes from verified backend data or is clearly labelled as demo content. We never fabricate shelter records, live prices, or donation outcomes.
